About Badminton Rally

Gear guides and training advice for club players

Badminton Rally publishes straight-talking gear reviews and training guides for club and league players — the kind of advice we wish existed when we were buying our first "real" racket.

No sponsored reviews dressed up as independent opinions. When we recommend a product, it's because it fits the price point and skill level we're writing for — and yes, some links are affiliate links, clearly marked as such.

Badminton Rally is built by the team behind Shuttle Life, a badminton coaching brand offering structured training programs and memberships.
